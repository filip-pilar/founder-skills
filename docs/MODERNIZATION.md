# Astra edition

Target: GPT-6 Astra. Based on [OpenAI's guidance](https://developers.openai.com/blog/rethinking-skills-and-prompts-for-gpt-6-astra): precise activation, progressive disclosure, outcome-oriented instructions, explicit completion and proportionate boundaries.

The collection now has eight workflows:

| Former entry | Current home |
| --- | --- |
| Storytelling | Copy & Story (`copywriter`) |
| Cold Outreach; Launch Plan | First Customers & Launch (`growth-playbook`) |
| Unblock; Weekly Review | Founder Decisions (`decision-coach`) |
| Video Starter | Content & Video Planning (`content-strategy`) |

Customer Discovery, Experiment Design, Product Design Critique and Product Scope remain distinct jobs. First Customers chooses a route and prepares outreach/launch material; Experiment Design specifies and interprets a test. Product Scope defines what to build; Product Design Critique evaluates how a screen serves its user.

The old root `skills/` paths move to `.agents/skills/` so Codex can discover them locally. Replace old installed copies to avoid duplicate activation. Retired site URLs redirect to their current workflow. Downloads contain the complete selected skill, references and example, with no external sibling-skill dependency.

Fixed three-day experiment windows, mandatory framework recital, universal direct-selling thresholds, compulsory weekly rituals and claims about older models have been removed from active guidance. Lecture transcripts remain historical source material. Worked examples are authored illustrations; no comparative model benchmark or improvement claim is implied.
