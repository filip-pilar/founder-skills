# First Customers & Launch

Choose the highest-value acquisition action for the founder's actual stage, product and constraints. Consider whether the bottleneck is reaching relevant people, activating them, delivering repeat value or getting paid. Use real metrics where supplied; mark estimates and unknowns.

Direct conversations are a useful early option, not a universal gate. Existing distribution, self-serve products and different buying cycles may justify other approaches. Prefer one coherent recommendation to an automatic list of ten tactics. Where research is available, verify named communities, programs or channel facts before relying on them.

For outreach, establish fit and a credible reason to contact the person, then draft a short relevant message. For a launch, deliver the audience, promise, channel sequence, usable messages and follow-through suited to the requested scope. Do not require a bio or feed redesign unless it affects that launch. Drafting never implies permission to send messages or publish.

Use [references.md](references.md) for funnel diagnosis and [example.md](example.md) for an early outreach case. When the core uncertainty needs a test, specify the hypothesis, observable result and decision it will inform; keep it proportionate to the acquisition decision. Finish with the recommended next action and what result would change it.
