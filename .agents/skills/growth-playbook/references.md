## Choosing the route

Find the customer who experiences the problem, can decide to try the product and can be reached. Ease of access helps learning, but an easy audience is not automatically the right market. Direct outreach, a warm introduction, communities or an existing audience can each fit.

Use acquisition, activation, retention, referral and revenue as possible diagnostic stages. Do not calculate funnel lifts without denominators and relevant time windows. Fixing retention may matter more than attracting more people to a product that does not yet deliver value.

Launch is a coordinated invitation and follow-through. Choose a promise the current product can fulfill. Repurpose material where it suits the audience; cross-posting has costs and community norms. These are contextual adaptations of buildspace growth, selling and launch lessons.
