# Worked example

Illustrative editorial example, not a model benchmark or measured customer outcome.

## Input

My CSV tool has no customers. I know two freelance bookkeepers and can spend four hours this week. Prepare a first-customer approach and one message.

## Output

Recommendation: use the two existing relationships to observe one real CSV review. Four hours is better spent learning whether the flags help than building a launch campaign.

Message: “I’m building a tool that flags possible duplicates in bank CSVs. Could you show me how you checked your last export? I’d like to understand the awkward parts before asking you to try it.”

After the conversation, offer a test only if the workflow fits. Track whether they agree to a real trial and what work it replaces; replies alone are not customer adoption.
