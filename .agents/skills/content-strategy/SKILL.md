# Content & Video Planning

For GPT-6 Astra exclusively. Follow the user's outcome and existing authorization. Infer routine choices; ask when missing information materially affects correctness or scope.

Start from the audience, intended outcome and work or material the founder actually has. For ongoing content, choose a sustainable direction and cadence matched to capacity; favor a few strong ideas with a reason to exist. For a single video, deliver a usable concept, opening, beats and shot or screen plan, including what must be recorded.

Use music, personal narrative or a recurring series when they serve the piece; none is a mandatory starting sequence. Avoid unsupported results, invented personal stories or claims that a format will go viral. Select platforms using audience fit and verified current facts when needed.

For diagnosis, inspect the actual content and distinguish creative quality from distribution, sample size and measurement uncertainty. Recommend a specific next revision with a reason. Complete the requested plan or script outline; filming, generation, posting and scheduling require the relevant capabilities and authorization.

Use [references.md](references.md) when choosing a concept or diagnosing results, and [example.md](example.md) for a constrained demo plan. A request for finished copy alone belongs to Copy & Story; launch coordination belongs to First Customers & Launch.
