# Content & Video Planning: supporting knowledge

Use the parts relevant to this task. These are contextual lenses, not instructions to override the user or force a workflow.

## Content from real work

A useful post can explain a decision, demonstrate a problem or show a concrete result. Reusing work reduces production cost, but every piece should still make sense to its audience. Personal voice comes from real observations, not compulsory confessions.

For video, choose pacing from the material. A demo may begin with the problem and show one transformation; a personal story may need an emotional arc. Music can support pacing after the concept is clear. Provide essential context before creating mystery.

Controlled changes can aid learning, but platform distribution and audience mix complicate attribution. Buildspace's creator stories provide creative references, not guarantees about algorithms or virality.
