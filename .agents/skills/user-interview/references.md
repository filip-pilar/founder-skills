# Customer Discovery: supporting knowledge

Use the parts relevant to this task. These are contextual lenses, not instructions to override the user or force a workflow.

## Questions that expose behavior

Ask about the last time the problem occurred, what triggered it, what the person did, what alternatives they tried and what it cost in time, money or errors. Follow an interesting answer rather than mechanically completing a script. “Would you use this?” is weaker evidence than an observed workaround or commitment.

A complaint and its proposed fix are different evidence. Preserve both, then investigate why the workaround exists. Distinguish segment differences from contradictions: a person with an automated import may have a different problem than someone handling CSV exports.

Direct conversation and careful interpretation are adapted from buildspace's user-feedback and authentic-selling material. No fixed interview count or rejection-rate target establishes validity.
