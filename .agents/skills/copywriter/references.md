# Copy & Story: supporting knowledge

Use the parts relevant to this task. These are contextual lenses, not instructions to override the user or force a workflow.

## Useful lenses

Clear → meaningful → persuasive is a diagnostic: first check comprehension, then relevance, then the reason to act. It is not a required response structure. The party test asks whether an unfamiliar reader can explain the product after reading the copy. Congruence means wording fits what the product actually does today.

For narrative, connect a real starting situation, a motivating event and a credible change. Personal details help when supplied and relevant. Match pacing to the reader and medium; a technical explanation need not use only short sentences. Specificity must come from evidence, not invented numbers.

These ideas are adapted from buildspace writing and storytelling lectures; examples in this package are new illustrative cases, not reported customer results.
